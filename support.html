Why

Three years back to songwriting

I started writing again three years ago after a long break from music. I think I needed the time away to figure out what I really wanted to say, to find my own voice, and to rediscover the part of music that made me fall in love with it in the first place.

In October 2025 I started releasing those songs. Since then I've put out three independent singles and, in 2026, started playing shows throughout Northeast Ohio. What began as finding my way back to songwriting has grown into something I care deeply about building.

Now I'm ready to make my debut EP — my first chance to create a full body of work that really represents who I am and the kind of artist I'm becoming.

I'm creating and funding this project independently. Between recording and production, mixing and mastering, and paying the musicians who will help bring these songs to life in the studio, a record like this is a significant investment. So I've decided to do something that feels a little vulnerable and invite you into the process.

There's no pressure to contribute financially. Whether you listen, share a song, come to a show, or support the record directly, I'm grateful you're here. Making music independently takes a village, and I feel lucky to be building mine.

The Record

Five original songs

This will be my first body of work — five songs that feel like the truest representation yet of the artist I've been working toward becoming.

I'll be investing in the project alongside you. If we go past the goal, every additional dollar goes back into the EP and its release. Throughout recording, I'll share glimpses behind the scenes as these songs come together.
