About

Abigail Royce

A soulful country artist from Ohio with a poet’s touch and a bit of edge. Blending Americana, Folk, and Southern Rock, she’s shaping a modern Midwest country sound that’s warm, gritty, and honest.

Her songs lean into vivid storytelling with a voice that’s equal parts honey and smoke. She turns real life into song in a way that’s raw, grounded, and hard to forget.
